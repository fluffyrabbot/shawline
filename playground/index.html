    Typing Shavian

    With Transliterate on, each letter has short keys that spell its sound, and the longest match wins: th is 𐑔. Type / between keys to keep them apart, = for the namer dot, and v outside a word for the ▼ arrowhead. Readings spell the schwa @ as ə. Each letter is listed with its keys and a word it sounds in.

      Rounded boxes are definitions, square boxes are nodes that call them, and wires carry values from sources to arrowheads. The drawing is the program.
